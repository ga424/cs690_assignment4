## Exit Criteria

- Business intent is captured.
- Scope and non-goals are explicit.
- Owners and approval authorities are identified.
- External anchors and human decision categories are identified for material claims and approvals.
- Data classification and regulated-domain applicability are explicit.
- National, multijurisdiction, global, or otherwise regulated jurisdiction triggers are inventoried, with unreviewed jurisdictions blocked by default.
- Model-context and MCP access policies are defined.
- Product outcome metric, maturity profile, and agent workflow boundary are defined.
- Technical delivery baseline, Definition of Ready, approved stack boundary, and codebase context scope are defined.
- Standards applicability matrix, binding authority, standards owners, exception path, and unresolved unknowns are defined.
- Security-first scope, threat-model need, vulnerability-risk assessment need, red/blue/purple need, risk appetite, and CISO/CRO/CFO decision path are defined.
- Open-source-first agent measurement stack posture, telemetry standard, eval/red-team gate scope, data controls, and exception authority are defined.
- Skill execution, handoff, co-work, parallelism, and loop expectations are defined or explicitly marked not applicable.
- Workflow affinity map connects stages, skills or agents, controls, evidence, validation, feedback, and authority.
- Initial assumptions, unknowns, and risks are recorded.
- Next stage is authorized or blocked with a clear reason.
